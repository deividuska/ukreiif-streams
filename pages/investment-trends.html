ABOUT

What is UKREiiF?

UKREiiF is the UK’s leading forum for real estate, investment and infrastructure – the place where the built environment community gathers to shape the future of places. Held in Leeds each May, it brings together 16,000+ delegates, including 4,000+ investors and developers, across three days, 60+ stages and 1,250+ speakers, with over £300bn of opportunities discussed.

For the Investment and Finance Trends & Insights stream, that scale matters: this is where pension funds, REITs, family offices, banks and institutional investors will come together to provide valuable insights into evolving market trends, investment opportunities, economic headwinds and emerging sector developments. Through a programme of events, the Investment and Finance Trends & Insights stream will give attendees the opportunity to gain expert market intelligence, share perspectives with peers and engage in discussions shaping the future of UK investment and development.
