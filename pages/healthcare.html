What is Healthcare Day @ UKREiiF?

Taking place on Thursday 20 May 2027 as part of UKREiiF in Leeds, Healthcare Day brings together the people, projects and partnerships shaping the future of health infrastructure and healthy cities across the UK. From NHS trusts and hospital modernisation programmes to healthcare real estate investment and government policy, the day connects the decision-makers driving one of the country’s most significant infrastructure sectors, with speakers including Karin Smyth MP, Minister of State for Health, alongside senior NHS England leadership.

Health is no longer treated as a standalone service challenge – it’s a core driver of placemaking, investment and long-term community value, and this day is where that thinking gets tested against real projects and real capital. Set within UKREiiF’s wider forum of over 16,000 delegates and £300bn+ in opportunities, Healthcare Day gives developers, investors and public sector leaders a focused space to build the partnerships behind the New Hospital Programme and the next generation of healthy places.
