What is High Streets & Urban Centres Day @ UKREiiF?

Taking place as part of UKREiiF in Leeds, High Streets & Urban Centres Day brings together the people, projects and partnerships regenerating town and city centres across the UK. From retail and hospitality operators and landlords to councils, BIDs, developers and investors, the day connects the organisations deciding what happens to the vacant unit, the tired precinct and the half-let parade – and how they become places people choose to spend time in again.

High streets are no longer a retail problem to be solved in isolation – they are where housing, leisure, culture, civic services and investment meet, and where the case for town centre regeneration is made to the brands and funders who can deliver it. Set within UKREiiF’s wider forum of over 16,000 delegates and £300bn+ in opportunities, High Streets & Urban Centres Day gives occupiers, local authorities, developers and investors a focused space to build the partnerships behind the UK’s next generation of urban centres.
