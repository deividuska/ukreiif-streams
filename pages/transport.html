ABOUT

What is UKREiiF?

UKREiiF is the UK’s leading forum for real estate, investment and infrastructure, and the place where the built environment community gathers to shape the future of places. It brings together 16,000+ delegates, including 4,000+ investors and developers, across three days, 60+ stages and 1,250+ speakers, with over £300bn of opportunities discussed.

For transport and infrastructure, that mix matters. Rail, roads, ports, airports, energy and digital networks decide which sites become viable and which places grow, and they depend on the same capital and the same public sector decisions as the development around them. The government departments, transport bodies, operators, infrastructure investors and delivery partners behind those networks are already in the room. The Transport & Infrastructure Stage is where they get into how projects are funded, sequenced and delivered.
