What is the Sports Investment & Regeneration Summit @ UKREiiF?

UKREiiF is the UK’s leading forum for real estate, investment and infrastructure, and the place where the built environment community gathers to shape the future of places. It brings together 16,000+ delegates, including 4,000+ investors and developers, across three days, 60+ stages and 1,250+ speakers, with over £300bn of opportunities discussed.

For sports-led regeneration, that scale matters. Stadiums, arenas and training facilities are anchoring new homes, commercial space and transport investment, and sport is one of the UK’s strongest cards with international investors. The investors, developers, local authorities and policymakers who decide what gets funded and built are already in the room. The Sports Investment & Regeneration Summit is where the case for sport gets made directly to them.
